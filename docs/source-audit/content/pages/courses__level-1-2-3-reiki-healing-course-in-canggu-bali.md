What is Reiki Healing?

Reiki is one of the most gentle but effective healing arts in humanity, which has its roots in the wisdom culture of early 20th century Japan, through the research of Mikao Usui. This is an amazing practice and it directs universal life force energy- the same vital energy that flows in all living creatures and links us to the immense wisdom of being.

Even its name explains its meaning: ‘Rei’ which means sacred or universal consciousness, and ‘Ki’ which means the life force energy that flows through all the cells of our body. Unlike other curing systems that take years of complicated training, Reiki functions under the beautiful concept of energy transmission where the healing power is passed naturally through the hands of the practitioner to the places where it is most required.

The remarkable thing about Reiki is that it is wise in nature – this healing power has an inherent wisdom that directs it exactly to those places of imbalance, be it physical, emotional, mental or spiritual. In a Reiki session, energy centres and organs are associated with certain positions of the hands of practitioners which form a sacred area where the process of deep healing can take its natural course.

The healing mechanism involves raising the vibrational frequency of the recipient, and it assists in dissolving energetic blockages that may be in the form of stress, illness, emotional storm or spiritual disconnection. When these blockages are released, the natural healing processes in the body are triggered and restoration takes place on all levels of being.
