An Insight into Reiki Healing: Background and Beginnings

Reiki is an age-old healing method that originated in Japan around the beginning of the 20th century. “Reiki” is derived from two Japanese words: “Ki” means energy, and “Rei” means universal life. So it means universal life energy. This intense energy healing was rediscovered by Dr. Mikao Usui during his search in the 1920s to discover a holistic way to health.

The philosophy behind Reiki is simple and profound- to channel the universal life energy to help clear obstructions in the body, mind, and spirit, where energy freely flows. This has won it recognition worldwide for being effective in reducing stress and pain with general health improvement as a major plus.

At Bali Yoga School, we embrace this ancient practice to guide you through your healing journey, combining traditional wisdom with a compassionate touch for a truly transformative experience.
