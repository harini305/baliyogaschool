5 Best Yoga Teacher Training Schools in Bali 2025
YOGA RETREAT IN UBUD BALI, YOGA SCHOOL IN BALI DECEMBER 26, 2024 BALI YOGA SCHOOL  SHARE

Bali has become one of the world’s leading destinations for yoga teacher training. The atmosphere of the island is calm, the climate is warm, and strong spiritual traditions make it an excellent place to study yoga deeply. A large number of young people who want to become yoga teachers come to Bali for its blend of traditional wisdom and modern teaching approaches, along with its affordable living costs and picturesque nature. When planning for the future to 2025, there are several yoga schools in Bali which offer very good training. These schools have the records of training competent yoga teachers who further teach yoga successfully in various parts of the world. Every school provides something in a different way, including the conventional teaching techniques and the innovative techniques. Here are the five best yoga teacher training schools in Bali for the year 2025.

1. Bali Yoga School

Situated in the religious core of Ubud, Bali Yoga School is a world-class yoga center for teacher training. Bali Yoga School integrates traditional knowledge about yoga with the contemporary methods in the 200 and 300 hours Yoga Alliance accredited courses. The large campus with Balinese style buildings and open air yoga halls set amongst beautiful gardens that provide an excellent atmosphere for learning and transformation.

What Bali Yoga School Offer:
Complete immersion in yogic studies through the carefully crafted curriculum covering asana, pranayama, meditation, anatomy and physiology and philosophy.
Expert guidance from Balinese and international trainers with over decades of teaching practice in different kinds of yoga.
Modern accommodations and organic vegan breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Small class sizes to ensure that students get individual attention and one on one with the mentor.
Teaching career resources and assistance for after graduation to kick start your teaching career.
Why Choose Bali Yoga School:
Decades of experience in training successful Yoga teachers across the globe.
Registered Yoga Alliance school with global accreditation.
Combination of time tested principles and principles of the new millennium.
Favorable location in the middle of Ubud, the island’s spiritual and art capital
Strong alumni network with teaching opportunities worldwide
Providing support and guidance even after the completion of the program

Bali Yoga School offers comprehensive asana training which includes proper alignment, adjustment and safe sequencing for all types of classes. They study the history of yoga, the meaning of the terms used in yoga practice, and meditation, as well as the contemporary approaches to teaching. The program focuses on teaching practice experience, providing students with the best chance to apply their skills in practice under the supervision of the experts. Daily routine is well planned starting with the meditation and pranayama, then asana practice and the methodology of teaching. Students are provided with three vegan meals every day which are made from fresh organic products sourced locally. The program also offers cultural components; Balinese experience, events, special classes, and healing sessions are offered throughout the course. Alumni relations are well developed, and the school continues to support graduates, as well as the international yoga community at large.

2. Himalayan Yoga Association

Despite its name suggesting Himalayan origins, the Himalayan Yoga Association has established a strong practice and spread the genuine message of traditional yoga in Bali. Their Bali center unites the knowledge of Himalayan yoga traditions and the power of the island.

The school’s major asset is its conservative method of teaching yoga. Their curriculum is mostly based on the eight limbs of yoga so that students are aware that yoga is not just the physical practice. Teachers of this association are usually trained in India, which makes them deliver truly yogic knowledge to the Balinese coast.

Their teacher training programs are special for the emphasis on shatkarma, pranayama, meditation, asana, yoga anatomy, yoga philosophy, proper alignment and adjustment – All the necessary parts of yogic learning. Students learn how to train their learners in different breathing exercises and meditation practices as part of the yoga practice. The school also focuses much on Sanskrit education and on the chanting of mantras so that the students will be able to understand the religion behind yoga.

It is a serene learning environment with all the mod cons while still retaining an ashram feel. Among them, there are specialized modules in Ayurveda, yoga therapy, sound and reiki healings which give students more instruments for their teaching practice.

3. Blooming Lotus Yoga

Blooming Lotus Yoga has rightfully earned its title of one of the most credible yoga teacher training centers in Bali as it offers a comprehensive approach to yoga education. The school is situated in an offbeat area, free from the noise of tourists which makes it an ideal place for learning and transformation.

Their teacher training program is considered to have an excellent grounding in the meditation as well as in the mindfulness. Unlike most other schools, Blooming Lotus incorporates a daily meditation and mindfulness practice and workshops into their curriculum, so the students are able to build up their own practice besides the teaching skills.

The teaching of asana at the school is systematic and structural because students must understand the techniques of teaching asanas and their correct form. Their qualified faculty includes such professionals as anatomy and philosophy teachers and pedagogy methodologists.

Where Blooming Lotus sets itself apart from other professional development companies is that they focus on the individual growth of the employee as much as they do on the corporate growth of the business. There are various accountability meetings which are in the form of group discussions, and one-on-one advising of the students to address challenges and foster the emergence of the students’ unique voice as teachers.

To know more, visit – https://www.blooming-lotus-yoga.com/

4. All Yoga Training

All Yoga Training has established itself as the innovative and evidence-based practitioner of traditional yoga. Their teacher training programs combine the original intentions of yoga with the current knowledge of human body and biomechanics.

They also focus on offering comprehensive information on how functional movement works in relation to yoga asana. Students are not only taught specific movements, but rather the reasoning and physics behind the movements and their safety. This knowledge-based approach increases the competitiveness of graduates in the current market demand of yoga teaching.

Organizations like All Yoga Training have set up training environments that encourage block-learning environments which are equipped with expansive practice areas, enhanced with proper training equipment. Their program features a tremendous amount of practice teaching sessions where student’s teaching performance is evaluated and critiqued by practicing teachers.

The school is also known for its business education part, which gives specific advice on how to start and develop a yoga teaching business. For instance, the course will cover areas such as the marketing communication, social media engagement and welfare standard within the contexts of the yoga industry.

To know more, visit – https://www.allyogatraining.com/

5. The Yoga Barn

The Yoga Barn in Ubud has expanded from just one studio to a facility that encompasses a wide range of services for yoga and teacher training. They are well reputed and have already set their roots in the Bali Yoga Industry, and therefore, are one of the best places to train to become an instructor.

The strongest point of The Yoga Barn is the variety of faculty members, who come from different backgrounds in yoga practice. Such exposure to various teaching techniques enables the students to come up with their own style of teaching yoga. The center’s facilities are multiple studios, healing center, and organic café, and their training programs utilize all of them.

The curriculum of their teacher training is quite balanced, including the historical features of yoga and the issues that are essential in modern classes. Vinyasa, Yin and Restorative yoga are taught to the students ensuring that the teachers are equipped to teach in any style.

The Yoga Barn is situated in the middle of Ubud town and is convenient to the many yoga enthusiasts as well as the cultural sites. It is enriched by guest speakers who are visiting yoga teachers and health and wellness instructors.

Conclusion

These five schools offer high-quality yoga teacher training in Bali’s supportive environment. Each school has its own teaching style and special focus, making it possible for students to find the right fit for their goals. When choosing a school, consider the teaching style, program content, and location that best matches your needs.

The schools maintain high teaching standards while offering their own unique approaches to yoga education. Whichever school you choose, studying in Bali provides an excellent foundation for becoming a yoga teacher. The combination of qualified teachers, peaceful surroundings, and comprehensive training programs helps students develop the skills and confidence needed to teach yoga effectively.
