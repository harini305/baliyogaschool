What is Reiki Healing?

Reiki healing is a very simple but at the same time, a powerful method that first started in Japan. The word Reiki itself is made of two Japanese words which are Rei means universal, and Ki means life force energy. Reiki is the natural energy that flows through every living being. Human beings can learn how to handle this universal energy and deliver it to others as well. If this energy flows freely, we feel healthy, happy, and calm, but if it is blocked then we feel stressed, weak or even sick.

The process of reiki healing is very gentle. The practitioner does not perform any massage or physical touch in reiki healing session. Instead, the energy is channeled through out hands with the hands lightly placed on or above the body. This energy flows naturally to where it is needed the most or even experienced healers can direct the energy to certain locations, helps the body and energy within to heal itself in a natural way. Reiki is not connected to any religion, so it is open for everyone regardless of beliefs or backgrounds.

In the first level of reiki training, people usually focus on healing themselves and learning the basics and foundations. But in the level 2 and level 3 reiki training, you go much deeper to an advance level. Here, we teach sacred Reiki symbols, distance healing techniques, and advanced methods such as healing techniques of specific targets. These higher levels are not only about practicing but also about learning how to live with more compassion, love, and opportunity to practice regularly to help you to become reiki masters.
