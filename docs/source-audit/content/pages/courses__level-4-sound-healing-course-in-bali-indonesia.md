Why Sound Healing?

Sound healing is the technique of balancing the body and the mind with the help of instruments such as Tibetan singing bowls, crystal singing bowls, gongs, and tuning forks. All cells, all organs, all thoughts have their own vibration, and the stress, fatigue or emotional burden can upset the balance, but sound can bring them back into balance.

At this advanced stage, that understanding moves beyond theory. You step into our yoga school in Bali not just only to receive these vibrations but to learn how to channel them with efficacy, intent and an increased level of accountability for the people you will eventually teach.

It is a more sophisticated program than it has been before, further developing all that you have accomplished up to now, and once again set in the natural splendor of Bali.

Who accompanies you this time? The same tools, but a different way of using them. The Tibetan and crystal bowls which you brought with a sense of curiosity are now familiar to you.

What is the purpose of returning? To enter the role of a facilitator fully in an environment of spiritual space, where healing happens not only for oneself, but for others.
