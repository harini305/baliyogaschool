Yoga Teacher Trainings in Uluwatu Bali
Welcome to Bali Yoga School in Uluwatu Bali

If you’ve been dreaming about deepening your yoga practice in a place that truly inspires, you’ve found the right place. Our Bali Yoga School in Uluwatu offers something truly special – to become a Yoga Alliance certified yoga teacher while surrounded by the natural beauty and peaceful energy that makes Bali so magical. We’ve chosen the perfect spot in Uluwatu for your yoga teacher training journey. The campus is located in a serene corner of Uluwatu, less than 15 minutes to some of the most beautiful beaches and sunset cliffs of Bali. Here you can have the best of both worlds: the serene, silent power that you require when you are in deep study and practice, yet not be isolated from all the goodness that Uluwatu has to offer.
During your break in your training, you can easily go to the heart of Uluwatu or find a short ride to the beach. The place is perfect as you are near everything, but not too near to the tourist crowds to actually concentrate on your practice and studies. What makes our yoga teacher training program special isn’t just our beautiful location, though. You’ll be learning from experienced teachers who’ve been walking the yoga path for years. They don’t just know the poses nor theories – they understand how to help you grow as both a practitioner and a future teacher. These aren’t just instructors; they’re mentors who genuinely care about your journey.

By staying here you are able to feel what it is like to live a yogic life. You will train with that great ocean air blowing through Uluwatu everyday. There’s something magical about doing sun salutations while actually feeling the warmth of the Balinese sun on this island with the waves in the distance.
And here’s a bonus – we’re very close to GWK Cultural Park, this incredible place with a massive running track and beautiful gardens. It is ideal to jog, or even explore when you need a change of scenery other than the beach.
Do not worry about feeling lonely in your training. Uluwatu has grown into this incredible mix of modern fusions and traditional Balinese culture. Trendy cafes with the best coffee and health oriented dishes, restaurants with local and international food and shops where you can buy whatever you need will be found. It is kind of having a small part of home in a completely new and motivating place.
This isn’t just yoga teacher training, it’s a complete experience that will change how you see yoga, teaching, and surely even yourself.
