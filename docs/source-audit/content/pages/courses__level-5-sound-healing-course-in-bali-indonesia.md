Level 5 Sound Healing Course Bali Indonesia

You have already mastered the art of working with singing bowls and the gong. Now, it is time to take the next step and learn how to share that knowledge with others. The Level 5 Sound Healing Course at Bali Yoga School in Ubud, Bali, is the highest level of the Sound Healing Arts program designed for those who wish to be confident and certified sound healing practitioners. 

This course is designed to prepare you to teach with clarity and confidence, unlike the previous levels which focused on building your practical skills. You will be taught how to simply explain sound healing methods and facilitate students’ learning process, as well as how to develop a conducive learning atmosphere. This training will enable you to have the ability to create structured courses, to coach future practitioners, and to be able to lead professional sound healing teacher training programs with confidence:

Curriculum Design and Teaching Methodology: Sound Healing and teaching sound healing are two entirely different things. In this one, you’ll learn how to simplify complex ideas for your beginner learner. You will understand how to structure a full program, how to time, and when to adapt your teaching style to different learners in front of you.

Mentorship and Facilitator Supervision: You will get practice at watching others during real sessions as they work to improve their confidence and then give them feedback on how to do this. It is this one skill more than any technique with the bowl or the gong, that distinguishes a good skiller from a respected sought after teacher.

Building & Running Your Own Sound Healing Programs: From pricing your workshop fairly to designing a complete retreat day with just a sound experience, this level will help you to take your practice seriously, and to see it as a real business instead of a one-time workshop.

At the end of this course, you will no longer be just a sound healer with one session daily. You are a person who can train, certify and support the next generation of facilitators who will continue this work.

Why Sound Healing?

Most people begin their sound healing journey because they are looking for peace, relaxation, or a deeper connection with themselves. They learn that sound can soothe the mind, alleviate stress and promote emotional and physical good health. Over time, this changes, as they keep practicing. Their healing attention gradually turns to giving it to others.

This course has been developed due to the fact that mere practical knowledge does not make a person an effective teacher. Many sound healers are very adept at creating beautiful sound healing ceremonies, but sometimes a student asks, "Why is that you use this bowl and not that one? What does the sound do? What does the meaning of this sound healing technique do? It is crucial for a teacher to be able to articulate their concepts concisely, with patience, and to distill complex concepts into simple and meaningful explanations.

In this training, you'll come to understand how to take your years of practice and formalize it into training. Will be comfortable answering questions and able to clearly explain ideas and detailed technique in steps and show how to learn it. The island of Bali has become one of the world's most renowned destinations for yoga, meditation and all forms of holistic healing practices. Thousands of students visit here every year to deepen their practice, reconnect with their own and to learn from the great teachers. It's the final step in your career in a peaceful environment in Ubud.

This level of training is different from the one you've taken before, where the emphasis was on acquiring knowledge about sound healing for yourself; in this level, the focus is on teaching others. You won't see these Tibetan singing bowls, crystal bowls and gong as new instruments. You already know how to work with them with confidence. Now it's on you as the player to make the changes.

This is the last level at Bali Yoga School where you can learn to become more than an experienced sound healer. It equips you with the skills and knowledge necessary to become a mentor, guide, and certified educator ready to confidently train future practitioners internationally. Congratulations for making it to the last step in the Sound Healing Arts journey. Here your knowledge can be shared, here you can inspire others, here you can contribute to the ever-expanding world of sound healing professionals.

What You Will Learn in the Level 5 Sound Healing Course in Bali
How to structure a complete sound healing curriculum, from Level 1 fundamentals all the way through Level 4 advanced practice.
How to run a teaching session correctly, ensuring that concepts are delivered without being overwhelming to a new student.
The essential elements of adult learning theory specifically for experiential, body based learning, such as sound healing.
Strategies for providing feedback to facilitators in training without hurting their confidence in the process.
How to know when a student is truly ready to move on, not because of the amount of time spent in the classroom, but rather in terms of demonstrated ability.
A step-by-step guide on how to create signature workshops, weekend intensives and full-retreat programs, all focused on sound.
The business basics of operating a viable sound healing practice, such as setting up prices for sessions, courses and full trainings that are fair and sustainable.
How and why group dynamics change once you leave the client and enter the student environment, and how to adapt your style to this change.
How to handle true challenging teaching moments: struggling student, distracting group, and an openly skeptical participant.
This is a more in-depth examination of the science of frequency, brainwave states and chakra theory from the perspective of a teacher.
Strategies to create emotionally safe learning environments for students for whom trauma-informed sessions are ongoing.
How to begin to develop your personal teaching voice and identity in the broader yoga and wellness community.
How to create meaningful working relationships with yoga studios, spas, and retreat centres, who want you to come train for them.
The ability to confidently teach full teacher trainings, rather than just individual sound baths, to both one-on-one and large groups.
The specific ethical responsibilities that come with teaching, including certification standards and ongoing student safety.
How to record and standardise their own teaching approaches, repeat and refine them year after year.
What to do when you don't know the answer to a valid question you can't answer right off the bat without losing the trust or attention of the room.
A clear, practical and realistic way to become a recognised sound healing teacher in Bali or anywhere in the world.
