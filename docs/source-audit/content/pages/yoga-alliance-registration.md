About Yoga Alliance USA

Yoga Alliance is a non-profit organization of yoga teachers based in the United States, established in 1999 as a voluntary registry of yoga teachers and schools. It has more than 7000 Yoga Schools and above 100000 Yoga Teachers all over the world today. The organization developed the Registered Yoga Teacher (RYT) designation of teachers who had attended a Registered Yoga School, setting the standard of education, codes of ethics, and professional standards that guarantee quality yoga education worldwide.

Why Global Recognition Matters

Yoga Alliance certification offers global credibility that is accepted in every corner of the globe, which has created openings to teachings across continents. It reflects professional standards and ethical practices by means of a recognized code of conduct. Wherever you go in Europe or Asia, Australia or the Americas, your certification is with you and you can find employment as a teacher. When students see instructors who have been trained in Registered Yoga Schools they are assured that they received high quality education.

Yoga Teacher Training Programs Covered

200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training (RYS 200) Our 200 Hour program enables you to build the basics of knowledge to start your teaching career on a high note. 300 Hour Yoga Teacher Training (RYS 300)

The advanced program is designed to enhance knowledge and teaching abilities and is offered to teachers with 200 hour certification. This is a four week training that has a prerequisite of 200 hour certification. When completed, on top of what you already have of 200 hours, you can be designated as RYT 500, which will indicate you are a highly trained advanced teacher.

500 Hour Yoga Teacher Training (RYS 500)

Our top certification level reflects mastery-level knowledge. Finish our full 500 hour intensive program or combine our 200 and 300 hour programs. The 500 hour certification grants you RYT 500 registration and, once you reach a certain number of teaching hours, E-RYT 500 which allows you to train other teachers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Yoga Alliance USA?

A: Yoga alliance is a non profit organization based in the United States which established Registered Yoga Teacher title to teachers who had their training in registered schools. It has voluntary registry systems that establish educational qualifications, moral principles and professional demands of yoga schools and teachers across the world.

 

Q: How do I register after graduation?

A: Go to the webpage of the yoga alliance, create an account, enter the certificate and choose Bali Yoga School as your training institution and send it to be verified. Pay the membership fee of $115 ( registration fee of 50 and annual dues of 65). We offer comprehensive instructions during the process.

Q: What’s the difference between RYS 200, 300, and 500?

A: The RYS 200 hour certification provides you with the entry level training. RYS 300 provides 300 hour programs to teachers who have already completed 200 hours certification. RYS 500 provides 500 hour programs which are done in totality or through a combination of 200 and 300 hour programs. Individual numbers are used to denote minimum training hours.

Q: Is Yoga Alliance certification valid internationally?

A: Yes! Yoga Alliance Certification is recognized all over the world. This means, you will get to teach in the whole world including Europe, North America, Asia, and Australia. These credentials give you the power to teach in the yoga studios, wellness centers and yoga retreats worldwide.

Q: Is this registration required to renew on an annual basis?

A: No expiration as far as your certification is concerned, but the Yoga Alliance registration is to be renewed annually (65 dollars). Remain qualified with 75 hours of continuing education complete after every three years, 45 hours yoga teacher, 30 hours training.

Q: Can I teach abroad with Yoga Alliance certification?

A: Absolutely! Your RYT qualification makes you credible and qualified to teach internationally. Countless locations and destinations particularly demand or give preference to teachers with Yoga Alliance certification to conduct leading classes, workshops, and retreats.

Q: Does Bali Yoga School help with registration?

A: Yes! We offer a full range of services such as pre-graduation training, timely certificate issuance (2-3 business days), fast verification, technical support, and guidance. We make sure that you do not have to leave as a student but become a Registered Yoga Teacher.
