8 DAYS & 7 NIGHTS YOGA RETREAT IN ULUWATU BALI

Give yourself the gift of stillness and sunshine with a soul-soothing escape to Uluwatu, Bali — where golden sands meet the calm rhythm of the sea. Immerse yourself in serenity as you unwind beneath the tropical sky, leaving behind the rush of daily life.

Join the best 8-Day Yoga Retreat in Uluwatu Bali, hosted by Bali Yoga School, one of the most trusted and authentic yoga centers on the island. Our retreat is thoughtfully designed to help you relax, reconnect, and recharge through the transformative practices of yoga, meditation, and healing therapies.

Nestled amidst lush greenery, our yoga retreat center features peaceful private rooms, two inviting swimming pools, and open garden spaces where you can soak in the sun or read your favorite book in tranquility. Each day unfolds with energizing yoga sessions, pranayama, meditation, and Hatha Vinyasa flow classes guided by our experienced teachers who blend ancient yogic wisdom with modern techniques.

You’ll also enjoy rejuvenating Balinese massages, detox and de-stress therapies, and holistic healing sessions that restore harmony to your body and mind. Whether you’re a beginner or an advanced yoga practitioner, our personalized approach ensures every guest experiences deep relaxation and self-growth.

When you’re not on the mat, explore the wonders of Uluwatu — from sacred cliffside temples and oceanfront cafes to scenic beaches and cultural charm. This retreat is your opportunity to pause, breathe, and rediscover balance in one of Bali’s most enchanting coastal havens.

At Bali Yoga School, your journey goes beyond yoga — it becomes a celebration of wellness, mindfulness, and inner peace.

Bali Yoga School Dining

We Bali Yoga School believe that food is a sacred expression of care and one of the ways to enhance our spirit. We serve is plant-based meals, prepared with the highest standards of hygiene, cleanliness, and love to support your yoga journey. One of the primary practices in yoga is ahimsa, meaning non-violence. By following a plant-based diet during your training, you are in the practice of ahimsa. Yoga is a way of living, and we bring that teaching in the meals as well. Our every dish reflects our commitment to provide the best yoga training experience, reflecting purity and mindful living. Our dedicated team prepares food with intention, mindfulness, and a deep sense of respect for the process. The energy behind the food matters just as much as the food itself and affect our physical, mental, spiritual health. It’s more than just food. It’s nourishment for your entire being. At Bali Yoga School, cleanliness is a reflection of inner purity. A pure plate leads to a peaceful heart. And every mindful bite is a step toward harmony.
