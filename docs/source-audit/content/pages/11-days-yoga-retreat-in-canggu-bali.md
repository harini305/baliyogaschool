11 Days Yoga Retreat in Canggu Bali

Thus , you can be assured of a ‘home away from home’ while at Bali Yoga School and with the best services provided by the Bali Yoga School family .  So get your act together as you add Canggu and Bali Yoga School to your bucketlist for this summer.
