The Comprehensive Curriculum: Complete Yogic Education

 

The 200 Hour yoga teacher training at Bali Yoga School incorporates all the necessary areas of yoga, providing a balanced base in teaching and practice.

Asana Practice

Students learn various traditional styles such as Hatha, Ashtanga and Vinyasa yoga. Every pose is dissected with attention, with body alignment, advantages, variations, and safe teaching techniques. This develops individual strength and instructional self-confidence.

Pranayama

The curriculum will contain strong breathing exercises such as Anulom Vilom, Bhastrika, and Kapalabhati. Students are taught the relationship of breath with body and mind and learn to feel the effects of different patterns which form various states of consciousness and offer means of dealing with stress and emotions.

Meditation and Mindfulness

There are guided visualizations, silent sitting, all types of meditation taught in daily meditation sessions. In the process of learning to introduce mindful awareness into everyday life and teaching, students create a sustainable practice of meditation.

Yoga Philosophy 

Physical practices have context in terms of study of classical texts such as Yoga Sutras and Bhagavad Gita. Students learn to use ancient knowledge in modern life, and yoga is not only an exercise, but a full spiritual practice.

Anatomy and Physiology 

Students get acquainted with the response of the body to yoga practices, muscles, joints, and the nervous system. This understanding avoids injuries, allows poses to be adjusted, and develops confidence in teaching in different populations safely.

Teaching Methodology

The training includes the practical teaching skills such as sequencing, clear instruction, and building inclusive environments. Teaching is a component of the program that students practice and learn how to adjust to various student needs and energy levels.

Arrival & Departure Dates:

The course starts on 1st of every month at 5:00 pm with the opening ceremony & orientation program, the course completes on 23rd of every month at 5:00 pm with farewell ceremony and 25th is last day and departure. In order for you to rest properly before the opening ceremony on the 1st day of the course, the check-in to the school should be one-day before the course begins. Your check in time must be between 10 AM to 4 PM. We provide you a complementary stay for that night. Your check out on the last day of the course must be between 12 PM to 4 PM.

What is the best way to arrive at Bali Yoga School ?

The best and most convenient way to travel for Bali is to take a flight to Ngurah Rai International Airport Denpasar Bali . Denpasar Bali airport is merely 38 kms from school.As soon as you book your flight, forward us your flight details and we will send our driver to receive you from Denpasar Bali airport.

Note : Complementary pick-up service from the DPS (I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport) is included in the course fee. Please provide your flight information once you book it, and we will arrange a pick-up for you.

TERMS, CONDITIONS & POLICIES
200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training in Bali - TOC & Privacy Policy

Please read the following course requirements, discipline rules and policies carefully before joining the 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training in Bali.

01
Class Attendance

All classes are mandatory. Only in case of an emergency or illness may a student take leave with permission from the respective teacher. Uninformed absences will not be accepted and may negatively affect the student's overall course performance.

02
Prerequisites

A high degree of self-motivation is required for all aspects of the course. The practice and especially the teaching of yoga demand a high degree of self-discipline and purity.

To ensure the success of the program, participants are required to attend all spiritual activities, meditation sessions, lectures and asana classes.

Meat, fish, eggs, black tea, coffee, alcohol, tobacco, drugs and nudity are prohibited during the course as they are considered counterproductive to yoga practice. Participants who do not comply with the school rules may be dismissed from the course.

03
Discipline Rules for Students

Smoking and alcohol are strictly prohibited at Bali Yoga School. If you are fasting on any day, please inform the kitchen manager in advance to help avoid food waste.

Students are expected to maintain discipline, respect teachers and follow all school rules. Please arrive on time for every scheduled class. Students who arrive late may not be permitted to join the class.

All outstanding accounts must be cleared before departure. Students must also return any books, maps or other items borrowed from Bali Yoga School before leaving.

Bali Yoga School provides accommodation for students enrolled in the yoga course. Friends or relatives are not included in the student's accommodation; however, additional accommodation may be arranged separately, subject to room availability and applicable charges.

Students are required to participate in all scheduled programs and activities included in the 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training in Bali.
