What is a Prenatal & Postnatal Yoga Training Course?
What is a Prenatal Yoga Training Course?

Prenatal yoga is a mindful, well-crafted yoga practice for and during pregnancy. It celebrates the amazing physical and emotional shifts that happen to women and supports them in making their transition through those transformations smoother, more conscious and more confident. While prenatal yoga is not a class where modifications are made, it’s its own style of yoga, which is created to develop strength, flexibility, to balance the emotions and to alleviate common discomforts experienced during pregnancy that are often found in a fitness class, such as anxiety, lower back pain, and fatigue.

Bali Yoga School’s prenatal yoga training is well-designed to meet the changing requirements of pregnant women throughout all phases of pregnancy, including the first trimester, the middle and late term, and the very end before birth. You will depart with the expertise to design safe, nourishing and effective classes for women at all points along this path.

What is a Postnatal Yoga Training Course?

As the baby arrives, the mother’s life doesn’t slow down; it plunges into a new and sometimes daunting world. The postnatal period has unique physical and emotional needs, hormonal changes, pelvic floor healing, bone tiredness and the silent emotional caliphate of new motherhood all at once. Many women say this is the period they felt most unprepared and least supported, which is often the most dramatic period of their lives.

Post Natal Yoga provides moms with a solid, natural approach to reconnecting with themselves. It is designed to restore core strength, supporting the recovery of the pelvis, easing the postpartum anxiety and gently reconnecting the inner balance needed for the woman to care for her baby and herself. It also gives her a space, and first space likely, where she is treated without demands and at her own speed, without being expected to do anything. 

Bali’s healing environment brings this process of restoration to a special level. This island is a place of tremendous power to learn postnatal care because the island’s rhythms, its unhurried pace, and its regard of life and birth is sacred. This training is not all about technique. It’s an empathy for what the newborn mom really wants and the capacity to provide for her. Post natal yoga is not just about getting back into shape after pregnancy, it is about recovery pursued through yoga. It’s a full return home!
