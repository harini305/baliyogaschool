What is Reiki Healing?

Reiki healing is a type of energy healing. It is also called alternative medicine. It is a Japanese technique that helps in stress reduce, relaxation and healing. The word “Reiki” comes from two Japanese words – “Rei” means “God’s wisdom” or “Higher power,” and “Ki” means “Life force energy.” So Reiki means “spiritually guided life force energy.”

A Reiki healer gives this energy by hands to another person’s body. The healer puts hands lightly on or just above the body, and the energy flows. It helps remove blockages, balance chakras, and bring back the natural flow of energy in body. This practice is soft and not harmful. It works on the idea that a life force energy flows in us and keeps us alive. If this energy is low, then people feel sick or stressed. If it is high, then we feel happy and healthy. The main aim of Reiki is to help body’s natural healing process and restore physical and emotional health.
