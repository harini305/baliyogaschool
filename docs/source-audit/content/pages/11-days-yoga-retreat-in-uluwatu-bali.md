11 DAYS & 10 NIGHTS YOGA RETREAT IN ULUWATU BALI

Take a deep breath and step into serenity with the 11-Day Yoga Retreat in Uluwatu, Bali, a transformative escape that nourishes your body, mind, and soul. While a holiday might help you unwind, a yoga retreat offers something deeper — true healing, balance, and self-discovery.

Hosted by Bali Yoga School, one of Bali’s most renowned yoga centers, this retreat combines ancient yogic wisdom with modern wellness practices to create a complete rejuvenation experience. Over 11 days, immerse yourself in daily yoga sessions, sound healing, reiki therapy, Ayurveda counselling, guided meditation, and relaxing Balinese massages.

Our peaceful retreat center in Uluwatu is designed for ultimate relaxation — featuring private rooms, two inviting swimming pools, and tropical garden spaces where you can lounge, read, or simply enjoy the ocean breeze. Each yoga class is personalized according to your experience level, whether you’re a beginner exploring the basics or an advanced practitioner deepening your practice.

Between sessions, explore the stunning surroundings of Uluwatu — from its sacred temples perched on dramatic cliffs to golden beaches and charming local cafes. You’ll also have the chance to take part in self-growth workshops that nurture mindfulness, clarity, and emotional balance.

At Bali Yoga School, we believe a yoga retreat is not just about movement — it’s a journey inward. Our 11-Day Yoga Retreat in Uluwatu invites you to slow down, connect with your inner self, and rediscover joy and peace in one of the world’s most beautiful destinations.

Bali Yoga School Dining

We Bali Yoga School believe that food is a sacred expression of care and one of the ways to enhance our spirit. We serve is plant-based meals, prepared with the highest standards of hygiene, cleanliness, and love to support your yoga journey. Yoga is a way of living, and we bring that teaching in the meals as well. Our every dish reflects our commitment to provide the best yoga training experience, reflecting purity and mindful living.
