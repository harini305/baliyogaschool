6 DAYS & 5 NIGHTS YOGA RETREAT IN ULUWATU BALI

Welcome to Bali Yoga School for the 6-days Yoga Retreat. This yoga retreat is much more than just daily yoga practices, but includes daily healing sessions (sound healing and reiki healing) and daily Balinese massage treatments. This yoga retreat is packed with yoga and healings, and at the same time, you can explore the center of Ubud and natural beauty of Bali in your free time as we are located very close to the Ubud city center and there are many waterfalls, forests, mountains to go on a hike.

Combine your holiday with yoga, and experience a totally rejuvenating holiday. We also have 2 swimming pools surrounded by the green in our retreat center, you can choose to stay and enjoy swimming or reading your favorite book by the pool under the shining sun.

Our retreat teachers area all very experienced to deliver yoga to people from beginners to advanced, according to each individual. We accept yoga teachers only who are authentic, have a heart to give, living with honesty. Our teachers are passionate about delivering the ancient knowledge and modern yoga teaching methods that can be applied to our daily life. Bali, especially Ubud is such an ideal place to get real yoga experience; it has a lot of healthy cafes that are vegan and vegetarian friendly, and has a lot of nature including the famous Balinese style rice terraces and Monkey Forest, spiritual events, and much more. We are always excited to welcome individuals who are interested in yoga, or simply wishing to have a rejuvenating time.

Bali Yoga School Dining

We Bali Yoga School believe that food is a sacred expression of care and one of the ways to enhance our spirit. We serve is plant-based meals, prepared with the highest standards of hygiene, cleanliness, and love to support your yoga journey. One of the primary practices in yoga is ahimsa, meaning non-violence. By following a plant-based diet during your training, you are in the practice of ahimsa. Yoga is a way of living, and we bring that teaching in the meals as well. Our every dish reflects our commitment to provide the best yoga training experience, reflecting purity and mindful living. Our dedicated team prepares food with intention, mindfulness, and a deep sense of respect for the process. The energy behind the food matters just as much as the food itself and affect our physical, mental, spiritual health. It’s more than just food. It’s nourishment for your entire being. At Bali Yoga School, cleanliness is a reflection of inner purity. A pure plate leads to a peaceful heart. And every mindful bite is a step toward harmony.
