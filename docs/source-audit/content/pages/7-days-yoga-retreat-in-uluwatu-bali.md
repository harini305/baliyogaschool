7 DAYS & 6 NIGHTS YOGA RETREAT IN ULUWATU BALI

Step into your zen zone with a transformative 7-day yoga journey in the breathtaking coastal paradise of Uluwatu, Bali. Known for its stunning cliffs, golden beaches, and soothing ocean breeze, Uluwatu sets the perfect scene for deep relaxation and self-discovery.

Join the best 7-Day Yoga Retreat in Uluwatu Bali, thoughtfully designed and hosted by Bali Yoga School, one of the island’s most respected yoga centers. Our peaceful retreat center sits amidst lush tropical gardens, featuring private rooms, two outdoor swimming pools, and tranquil open spaces where you can reconnect with nature and yourself.

Each day begins with rejuvenating yoga and pranayama sessions followed by guided meditation, detox therapies, and soulful Hatha Vinyasa flow practices. You’ll also enjoy healing experiences including sound therapy, reiki sessions, and traditional Balinese massages to help you unwind and restore inner balance.

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced practitioner, our experienced teachers blend ancient yogic wisdom with modern teaching methods to guide you toward physical vitality and mental clarity. In your free time, explore Uluwatu’s nearby beaches, ocean temples, surf spots, and hidden cafes — each offering a taste of Bali’s vibrant culture and natural beauty.

At Bali Yoga School, every retreat is a journey inward — a time to pause, breathe, and rejuvenate your spirit. Our 7-day yoga retreat in Uluwatu is more than a getaway; it’s a complete renewal for your body, mind, and soul.
