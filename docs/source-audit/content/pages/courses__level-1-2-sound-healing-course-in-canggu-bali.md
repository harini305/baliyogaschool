What is Sound Healing?

Sound healing is an ancient practice that use vibration, tones, and frequencies to restore balance in the body, mind, and emotions. Everything in the universe have a vibration, including our own body and thoughts. When these vibrations are disturb, we feel stress, sickness, or imbalance. Sound healing help to bring these vibrations back into harmony. In this method, special instruments are used like Tibetan bowls, crystal bowls, gongs, chimes, and tuning forks. The sounds from these tools goes deep inside the body and touch the cells, tissues, and energy centers. The result is relaxation, stress release, and natural healing.

Sound healing also affect our brainwaves. It help us move from busy, stressed state into alpha and theta, which are similar to deep meditation or rest. In this state, the body activate its natural repair system. That is why many people feels light, peaceful, and fresh after a session.

This practice is gentle and safe. You don’t need to know music or play instruments before. Just by learning to create and receive these vibrations, you can already bring powerful changes. In the Level 1/2 course, you understand how sound can heal and how to use it for yourself and others.
