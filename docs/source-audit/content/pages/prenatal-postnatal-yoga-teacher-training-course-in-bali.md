Prenatal & Postnatal Yoga TTC
Step into the most nurturing journey of your life, with Bali Yoga School

Pregnancy is one of the most profound and sacred transformations a woman will ever experience. It is lovely, strong and very sensitive simultaneously. But, in a surprise finding, only a very low number of pregnant women engage in any form of structured physical practice, including yoga, during their prenatal or postnatal care. This absence not only affects the well being of the mother, but also the growth and development of the child she carries.

At Bali Yoga School, we recognised this important gap and felt called to address it. The Prenatal and Postnatal Teacher Training Course is a specialised course, educating yoga teachers, healthcare workers and mothers in knowledge, skills and confidence to help women safely and effectively during all phases of pregnancy and early motherhood.

This program is to all those who think that a mother deserves professional care, profound support, and a practice that indeed respects where she is in her path. Because the one who nurtures all others must first be nurtured herself.

What is a Prenatal Yoga Training Course?

Prenatal yoga is a mindful and specially adapted yoga practice designed for the time of pregnancy. It celebrates the incredible physical and emotional transformations that occur in the body of a woman and helps her to journey through pregnancy in a more powerful, comfortable and conscious way.

A woman must have a practice that is equally gentle and empowering during pregnancy; one that cultivates physical strength, addresses discomfort, and develops emotional stability. Prenatal yoga does just that.

What Is a Postnatal Yoga Training Course?

The birth of a baby is one of the best gifts in life, yet to a mother, the journey does not end when the baby is born. The postnatal period is an extremely physically and emotionally changed period, which requires careful, informed treatment and regular attention.

Our postnatal yoga courses aim to ensure women slowly and gradually regain strength, reestablish pelvic wellness, balance their mood, and reidentify themselves following delivery.

What the Pregnancy Yoga Course Will Cover

Yoga and Pregnancy Philosophy: Students learn the philosophy of yoga in relation to pregnancy, and in this beautiful period of life they can learn about the oneness of mind, body and spirit.

Anatomy and Physiology of the Reproductive System: A close look at the physical and physiological changes which occur in the reproductive system during pregnancy.

Postnatal and Prenatal Trimester-Specific Asanas: A yoga practice made up of well-selected yoga postures designed to address the special needs of each phase.

Health and Nutrition During Pregnancy: Students are taught the value of nutrition for mother and child to ensure health and well being.

Garbhopanishad Study: A comprehensive study of this classical document on the development of the unborn child and the sacrality of life.

Pranayama during the Prenatal Phase: Breathing practices that support comfort, emotional balance and connection with the child.

Pre and Postnatal Care Psychotherapy: Awareness of emotional and psychological aspects of pregnancy and postpartum healing.

Teaching Methodology: Intensive training on how to design, organize and deliver effective prenatal and postnatal yoga classes.

Why Choose the Pre & Postnatal Yoga TTC at Bali Yoga School?

A Completely Holistic Curriculum: Our course includes anatomy, breathwork, meditation, nutrition and teaching methodology in the perspective of prenatal and postnatal needs.

Skilled and Caring Teachers: Students are taught by seasoned yoga instructors and trained medical practitioners.

Practice-Based Learning: Students engage in real-life instruction, case studies and scenario learning.

Community Learning: Students connect with others who share an interest in maternal well-being.

Completion Certification: Graduates receive a recognised certification during a special certification ceremony.

Who The Course Is For?
Pregnant women in the preconception or prenatal stages.
Parents who wish to undergo this process together.
Yoga teachers interested in prenatal and postnatal teaching.
Therapists, nutritionists and Ayurveda practitioners.
Any learner with or without prior yoga experience.
