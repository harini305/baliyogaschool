What is Sound Healing?

Ancient form of healing, Sound Healing was developed using vibrational frequencies of sound to help humans recover their physical, emotional, and spiritual wellness. Developed from the traditional philosophies of India and Tibet, among other old nations, sound healing functions according to the principle that everything in our body cells, organs, and systems resonate within their own frequency.

These instruments include the singing bowls, gongs, tuning forks, and chanting, which all create these resonant frequencies that help unblock, restore balance to, and uplift your total state of being. Whether for stress relief, mental clarity, or deep relaxation, sound healing acts as the holistic gateway to inner peace.

A Look at the History of Sound Therapy

Sound was first used for healing for over a thousand years.

Tibetan and Himalayan Singing Bowls: Ancient monks from Tibet used singing bowls in their meditation and healing rituals to generate soothing resonant tones to raise their spiritual consciousness.

Nada Yoga: In India, Nada has been very significant in yoga, therefore indicating the connection between the use of sound and being conscious.

Ancient Greek Traditions: Pythagoras among other thinkers, referred to “music as therapy” and thus was advocating the balance of the body and spirit.

Throughout the years, sound healing has been progressing but the basic purpose of it is still the same: bring back balance to the body using vibrations.
