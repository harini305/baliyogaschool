Ignite your passion and fulfill your aspirations to become an inspirational yoga teacher through an empowering program on 300 hours yoga teacher training in Canggu Bali. Bali Yoga School invites all yoga enthusiasts to Bali to experience the best of yoga and travel in Canggu – the next emerging hotspot of Bali after Ubud, for yogi travelers from all over the world.  It is here that you can witness a peaceful village atmosphere which also serve as a hideaway for those seeking zen and who prefer the proximity to the ocean to central Bali.

 

There are also cozy cafes and restaurants to lounge around and refresh your mind when not attending any classes during your stay for the 300 hours Yoga teacher training in Canggu Bali.  Besides, while being in Canggu,  in addition to yoga, surfing, Balinese massage, watching the sunset, temple visits, hiking, horseriding, and scuba diving are some of the other activities to kill boredom and monotony and do something different from your conventional activities back home. So here is a coastal zone promising you plenty of  “me-time”, loads of enjoyment and relaxation, and favorable environment to take your learning and practice of yoga to a professional level.

 

And to maximize your experience, Bali Yoga School has the perfect package that awaits you in the form of fun-filled excursions, ayurvedic cooking classes, kirtan nights, Satsang sessions and so much more in addition to the course on 300 hours yoga teacher training in Canggu Bali. So be a part of the Bali Yoga School family and grab yourself a rewarding opportunity of a lifetime!

 

After completing 300 Hour yoga teacher training, you will be able to teach the beginner, intermediate and advanced level yoga students with confidence. The entire program is designed by top yoga experts of Bali Yoga School. We train yoga teachers to become independent, wise and flexible and you will be prepared to deal with the most challenging class, special needs and health issues of the clients.

 

Important Points

 

Course includes advance practices of yoga, advance asanas, advance pranayama, advance yoga philosophy, advance yoga anatomy & physiology, yoga therapy and other subjects.

Arrival & Departure Dates

Our 300-hour Yoga Teacher Training in Canggu, Bali begins on the 1st of every month at 5:00 PM with an opening ceremony and orientation. The course concludes on the 6th of every month. To ensure you are well-rested before the training begins, we recommend arriving one day earlier. Check-in is available between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM, and we offer a complimentary night’s stay before the course starts. On the final day, check-out should be completed between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM.

The closest and most convenient airport is Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), Denpasar, located approximately 20 km from our Canggu campus. Once you have booked your flight, simply share your arrival details with us, and we will arrange an airport pickup.

Note: Complimentary pickup from DPS (I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport) is included in your course fee. Please send us your flight information after booking so we can organize your transfer smoothly..

Prerequisite Assignment for the 300 Hour Yoga Teacher Training Course

All students joining our 300-hour Yoga Teacher Training are required to complete the prerequisite assignment before arrival and submit it during check-in. Please print the assignment and compile it into a file for submission. This process ensures that all participants begin the training with a strong foundation for a meaningful and enriching learning experience.

Upon confirmation of your 300-hour YTT booking, we will provide the assignment questions along with our 200-hour YTT course manual. If you are unable to complete the assignment due to limited time before the course starts, you may submit it within the first 7 days of the training.

To ensure the success of the program, participants are required to attend all spiritual activities, meditation sessions, lectures and asana classes. Meat, fish, eggs, black tea, coffee, alcohol, tobacco, drugs and nudity are prohibited during the course as they are counterproductive to the yoga practice. Participants who do not comply with the school rules may be dismissed from the course.

Clear your books of account before departure from Himalayan Yoga Association. Before departure return your books, maps or any goods which you borrowed. Himalayan Yoga Association provides accommodation for a student who join the yoga course. So any friends or relatives will not be included in accommodation however they can stay in school by renting another room. Student have to be present in all scheduled program of Himalayan Yoga Association.

Refund Policy – The course fees & Booking amount will not be refundable, only in the case of emergency students can join us on other schedules. If student cancel the course after joining it, we accept cancellation but course fees will not be refund in cancellation.
