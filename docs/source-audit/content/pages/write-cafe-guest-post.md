Frequently Asked Questions
Can anyone submit a guest post?

Yes, we accept submissions from writers, bloggers, businesses, and hospitality professionals from all walks of life, as long as the content is editorially appropriate and value-add to readers.

Do you accept sponsored articles?

Sponsored content is accepted here, as long as it isn't strictly promotional, but rather still has a beneficial, informative, and relevant message for our audience.

Can I include backlinks?

Yes, if you choose to publish an article it will have dofollow backlinks, the exact number of dofollow backlinks depends on the package you have selected.

Do you accept AI-generated articles?

No, we only publish material which is researched and written by a human author, because this guarantees editorial consistency.

Can I republish my article elsewhere?

No, we need exclusive content that hasn't been published anywhere else before or after we publish it.

What is the time frame for the review?

Most of the submissions are considered within 2 to 5 business days, but sometimes longer for more in-depth and technical submissions.

Will my article be edited?

Yes, the text might be slightly modified with regards to grammar, formatting, readability and SEO structure, but without changing the actual meaning of the content. .

What happens if my article is rejected?

If the article is not of our editorial standards, then the article will not be published. If editorial work is not commenced, the payment will be refunded according to our refund policy.

Do you provide SEO optimization?

Yes, all accepted articles are correctly formatted and optimized to be search engine friendly as part of our normal publication procedure.

How do I submit my article?

Send your draft along with author details, website link and preferred package through our website or directly to our editorial team via email. .
