What is Reiki Healing?

Reiki Healing is an energy healing method that help to balance body, mind and spirit. It come from Japan, and the word “Rei” mean universal wisdom while “Ki” mean life force energy. Together, Reiki mean “spiritually guided life force energy.”
The practice of Reiki is simple but very powerful. A Reiki healer transfer this energy by gently placing hands on or just above the body. The energy flows naturally, helping to remove blockages, balance chakras, and bring back the natural flow of life force inside the body. When this energy is high, you feel healthy, positive, and active. When it become low, you feel stress, sickness, or tired.
Reiki is not only for physical healing. It also touch the emotional and mental levels. With practice, many people experience deep relaxation, clarity of thoughts, and better connection with their spiritual side. Reiki works soft, it is non-invasive and safe, and anyone can receive it without side effects.

In Level 2/3 course, you will also learn advance methods – such as Reiki symbols, distant healing, and how to guide the energy for specific needs. This make you able to use Reiki not just for self-care, but also to support healing for other peoples.
