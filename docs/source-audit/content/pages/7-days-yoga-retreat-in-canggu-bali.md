7 Days Yoga Retreat in Canggu, Bali

 

Experience the Complete Yogic Journey with our 7 Days Yoga Retreat in Canggu, Bali. Welcome to Bali Yoga School signature week-long immersion, where you’ll experience every facet of our innovative retreat program and emerge with profound understanding of authentic yogic living.

This is a yoga retreat reimagined. Unlike the traditional programs which put you on a strict timetable, our ground breaking flexible approach allows you to start your seven-day trip on any day of the week. Begin Sunday with light Surya Bhedi pranayama and body scan or start Wednesday with beach sunset flows and chakra work. Every starting place makes a different tapestry of growth, and your experience is really individual.

The seven days will give the benefit of true, deep integration, which is impossible in shorter retreats. You will learn all the seven pranayama techniques we offer- starting with the basics of Anulom Vilom that soothes your nervous system all the way to the advanced technique of Kapalabhati that helps to light the fire inside you. Feel the full range of soft Hatha yoga classes where you can go to deep healing and restorative sessions to hard Dynamic Hatha Vinyasa classes where you can develop un-shakable strength and elegant vitality. It is the time in which one can truly develop skills, have no apprehension of working independently, and be able to embody ancient wisdom permanently.

Your meditation experience will cover the whole spectrum of the conventional practices: sacred Om and potent Shiva mantra sessions, transformative chakra awakening sessions, mindful walking meditation in the temple grounds and tropical gardens, breath awareness practice, heart-opening loving-kindness practice, and deep body scan experiences that will free years of stored tension. Several beach sunset yoga sessions in the legendary beaches of Canggu leave soul-throbbing memories that keep on inspiring you even after going back home.
The seven-day experience will consist of a lot of Yoga Nidra and sound baths which will help to experience the most profound layers of rest and understanding. You have not only mastered yoga by your last day, but you have also adopted the whole lifestyle of conscious living.
This is your invitation to the ultimate transformation. Seven days to discover who you truly are beneath life’s layers.

Bali Yoga School Dining

We Bali Yoga School believe that food is a sacred expression of care and one of the ways to enhance our spirit. We serve is plant-based meals, prepared with the highest standards of hygiene, cleanliness, and love to support your yoga journey. One of the primary practices in yoga is ahimsa, meaning non-violence. By following a plant-based diet during your training, you are in the practice of ahimsa. Yoga is a way of living, and we bring that teaching in the meals as well. Our every dish reflects our commitment to provide the best yoga training experience, reflecting purity and mindful living. Our dedicated team prepares food with intention, mindfulness, and a deep sense of respect for the process. The energy behind the food matters just as much as the food itself and affect our physical, mental, spiritual health. It’s more than just food. It’s nourishment for your entire being. At Bali Yoga School, cleanliness is a reflection of inner purity. A pure plate leads to a peaceful heart. And every mindful bite is a step toward harmony.

Founder of Bali Yoga School

Yogi Himanshu

Everything you experience at Bali Yoga School community has come from our great leader, Yogi Himanshu Joshi. He is the most honest, hard-working, authentic person we can ever meet.
