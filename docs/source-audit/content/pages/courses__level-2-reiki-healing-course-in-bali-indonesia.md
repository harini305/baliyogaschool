At its core, Reiki is a spiritual practice that originated in Japan in the early 20th century. The word “Reiki” is a combination of two Japanese words: “rei,” meaning universal, and “ki,” meaning life energy. The belief underlying Reiki is that a universal life force energy flows through all living things, and disruptions or imbalances in this energy can lead to physical or emotional ailments.

Reiki sessions often begin with a brief discussion between the practitioner and the recipient, where the individual’s specific concerns or areas of focus are addressed. Origin of Reiki:

The origins of Reiki can be traced back to the early 20th century in Japan, with its development credited to a man named Mikao Usui. Reiki, as a healing practice, is deeply rooted in Japanese spiritual and esoteric traditions, and its evolution is closely tied to the life and teachings of Usui.

Mikao Usui was born on August 15, 1865, in the village of Taniai in the Yamagata district of Gifu prefecture, Japan. From an early age, he showed an interest in spiritual matters and sought to understand the mysteries of life and healing. Usui’s journey led him to study a variety of subjects, including Buddhism, Taoism, Shintoism, and the esoteric practices of Kiko (a Japanese form of Qi Gong) and Onmyodo (a traditional Japanese esoteric cosmology).

In his adult life, Mikao Usui became a Tendai Buddhist monk and pursued a career as a public servant and businessman. His diverse background and experiences laid the foundation for the synthesis of knowledge that would later become Reiki.
