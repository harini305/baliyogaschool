RETREAT PHILOSOPHY

This Retreat Is About Doing Less—More Mindfully

This is not an intensive yoga programme. The experience follows a slower arc: gentle movement prepares the body, conscious breath settles attention, sound encourages receptive stillness, massage supports physical relaxation, and unscheduled time lets the day land naturally.

Spaciousness is not an empty part of the timetable—it is part of the retreat. Every day protects time for the pool, reading, journaling, exploring Ubud, taking a nap or simply doing nothing.

Yoga Without the Rush. Breath Without Pressure.

RESTORATIVE YOGA

Yoga Without the Rush

This retreat feels different from Vinyasa or intensive yoga programmes. Movement is slow, postures can be supported, transitions are conscious, and relaxation receives as much importance as the shapes themselves.

You do not need to be flexible, experienced or physically advanced. Teachers can offer accessible options for complete beginners while experienced practitioners explore a quieter way of practising.

BREATHWORK

Come Back to Your Breath
