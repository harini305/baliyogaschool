100 Hour Yoga Teacher Training in Uluwatu Bali

Looking for a fun, real yoga adventure? Come to Bali! Skip the busy spots and head to Uluwatu, a stunning coastal area loved by yogis worldwide. Join Bali Yoga School’s 100-hour yoga teacher training here. This course is the first step in a 200-hour program. You’ll learn how to teach yoga safely, understand its benefits and safety tips, and enjoy a mix of theory and practice—with plenty of time to relax too!

Uluwatu is famous for its beautiful beaches, cozy cafes, and top-notch yoga studios, making it the perfect place to learn. Bali Yoga School focuses on traditional yoga styles like Hatha, Ashtanga, and Vinyasa. You’ll explore how yoga connects your mind, body, and emotions. Finish the course with a certificate to start your yoga teaching career.

The full 200-hour program goes deeper into mantras, poses, breathing exercises, meditation, and yoga philosophy. Classes are designed to balance learning and rest, so you stay energized. Start your yoga journey in Uluwatu—transform your practice and become a confident teacher.

Course certification-
Our all courses are yoga alliance USA certified that enables everyone to teach anywhere around the world as a certified yoga teacher. 

Dress code for opening and graduation ceremony-
Keep 1 or 2 pairs of white clothes for the opening and graduation ceremony of the course.

Important Points:

1- Our 100-Hour Multi Style Yoga Course is suitable for all levels, including beginners, intermediate practitioners, and advanced students.

2- We cover only the course syllabus that is specifically mentioned on the course page.
