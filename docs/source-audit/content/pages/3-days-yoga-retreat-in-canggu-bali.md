3 Days Yoga Retreat in Canggu, Bali

Transform Your Weekend into a Life-Changing Experience with our 3 days yoga retreat in Canggu, Bali. Sometimes the most profound transformations happen in the briefest moments. Our 3-day yoga retreat offers you the perfect escape from routine without requiring a lengthy commitment. Located in the vibrant beach paradise of Bali, where ancient temples blend with unlimited ocean horizons, this is a small but full-packaged program that offers the greatest impact in the shortest time possible.

What sets BYS so unique is our revolutionary flexible schedule program- you can choose your day of starting one of our many-weekly programs and create your own unique experience, which suits your schedule. You can come on Monday and have a relaxing Hatha yoga and Om mantra meditation, or you can come on Thursday and have an invigorating Vinyasa flow with Shiva mantra practice, every combination will be a retreat of your own energy and intentions.

You can spend your three days in magic of sunset beach meditation when waves gently move the sand at the dynamic beaches of Canggu, the richness of the Yin yoga and sound baths, or the awakening of certain pranayama exercises such as energizing Kapalabhati or balancing Nadi Shodhana. Every morning begins with purposeful breathing and conscious movement and afternoons offer a range of different meditation practices like chakra balancing, loving-kindness meditations, and body scans.

What is so lovely about our three-day format is its compressed intensity. You will quickly leave the stress of modern living and enter the eternal flow of the true yogic practice. Discover walking meditation in temples, feel the vibrations of traditional mantra chanting and get acquainted with ultimate relaxation of Yoga Nidra under tropical skies. One day leads to the other and creates the momentum of sustainable change.

This isn’t a regular yoga retreat, it’s a concentrated dose of ancient wisdom delivered in paradise. Ideal for busy professionals, interested beginners, or anyone who needs a meaningful reset without taking long breaks. Within 72 hours, you will be exposed to the entire gamut of yogical practices and at the same time you will be treated to the legendary hospitality of Bali and the natural beauty of the place. Your change starts the moment you step into our Canggu sanctuary, as every breath brings you closer to your true self.
