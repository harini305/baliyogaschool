9 DAYS & 8 NIGHTS YOGA RETREAT IN ULUWATU BALI

A regular holiday may help you unwind, but a yoga retreat in Bali offers something far deeper — a true sense of peace, renewal, and balance. Step into a world of healing and harmony with the 9-Day Yoga & Meditation Retreat in Uluwatu Bali, thoughtfully curated by Bali Yoga School, one of the island’s leading yoga centers.

Nestled in the serene coastal beauty of Uluwatu, our retreat center provides the perfect blend of comfort and tranquility. Enjoy private rooms designed for relaxation, two outdoor swimming pools surrounded by tropical gardens, and peaceful open spaces where you can meditate, read, or simply bask in the golden sunlight.

Each day begins with energizing yoga sessions followed by guided meditation, pranayama, and Hatha Vinyasa flow practices led by our experienced and compassionate teachers. Our instructors blend ancient yogic wisdom with modern techniques to help you detoxify the body, calm the mind, and awaken inner awareness — regardless of your age or level of experience.

Beyond yoga, you’ll experience a variety of healing therapies, including Balinese massages and self-improvement workshops. Between sessions, explore the magic of Uluwatu — its breathtaking beaches, sacred temples perched on cliffs, and vibrant local culture.

The 9-Day Yoga Retreat in Uluwatu is more than just a getaway — it’s a journey toward holistic wellness. Whether you seek to recharge, reflect, or simply reconnect with yourself, Bali Yoga School invites you to discover peace, joy, and renewal in the heart of paradise.

Bali Yoga School Dining

We Bali Yoga School believe that food is a sacred expression of care and one of the ways to enhance our spirit. Yoga Retreat Terms & Conditions

Booking Process

In order to book for retreat kindly deposit the Advance booking amount of 150 USD, Balance should be paid on arrival either by card, cash or Online transfer, An official confirmation letter concerning your program will be send to you shortly after successful advance booking fee.

Refund Policy

Booking deposit is refundable only when the customer cancels the course more than 15 days before the course starting date. The booking deposit well be refunded after deducting the amount of 10% from the deposit as the service charge + bank charge + the government tax. The refund will be initiated within 7 working days upon the request. If you cancel the course within 15 days from the course starting date, the deposit is not refundable. In this case, we accept date rescheduling.
If you cancel the course after joining, we accept the cancellation but the course fees will not be refunded with the cancellation.
