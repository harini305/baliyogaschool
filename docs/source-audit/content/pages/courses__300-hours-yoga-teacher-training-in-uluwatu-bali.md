300 Hour Yoga Teacher Training in Uluwatu Bali

 

Uluwatu in Bali—with its breathtaking cliffs, peaceful vibes, and sunny beaches—is the perfect place to deepen your practice. Join Bali Yoga School’s 300 hour yoga teacher training here and master traditional styles like Hatha, Ashtanga, and Vinyasa in a paradise loved by yogis worldwide.

This advanced course builds on your 200 hour training, diving deeper into poses, breathing, meditation, mantras, and yoga philosophy. Learn to teach with confidence, design classes, and understand body alignment. Uluwatu’s calm energy and stunning scenery (think cozy cafes, world-class studios, and ocean views!) help you focus and grow.

Our easy-to-follow classes mix theory and practice, with time to relax and reflect. You’ll graduate as a skilled, certified yoga teacher, ready to inspire others. Whether you want to level up your teaching or connect deeply with yoga’s wisdom, Uluwatu’s magic and our supportive training will transform you. Start your 300-hour adventure today—and become the teacher you’re meant to be!

Prerequisite Assignment for the 300 Hour Yoga Teacher Training Course

All students joining our 300-hour Yoga Teacher Training are required to complete the prerequisite assignment before arrival and submit it during check-in. Please print the assignment and compile it into a file for submission. This process ensures that all participants begin the training with a strong foundation for a meaningful and enriching learning experience.

Upon confirmation of your 300-hour YTT booking, we will provide the assignment questions along with our 200-hour YTT course manual. If you are unable to complete the assignment due to limited time before the course starts, you may submit it within the first 7 days of the training.

Accommodation

All our accommodations are private rooms with A/C to make sure you get proper rest and privacy to renew yourself each day during your yoga teacher training. Unwind by the swimming pool, or devote time to self-study in peaceful common areas. Our management team is dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of cleanliness and comfort.

Bali Yoga School Dining

We Bali Yoga School believe that food is a sacred expression of care and one of the ways to enhance our spirit. We serve is plant-based meals, prepared with the highest standards of hygiene, cleanliness, and love to support your yoga journey. One of the primary practices in yoga is ahimsa, meaning non-violence. By following a plant-based diet during your training, you are in the practice of ahimsa. Yoga is a way of living, and we bring that teaching in the meals as well. Our every dish reflects our commitment to provide the best yoga training experience, reflecting purity and mindful living. Our dedicated team prepares food with intention, mindfulness, and a deep sense of respect for the process. The energy behind the food matters just as much as the food itself and affect our physical, mental, spiritual health. It’s more than just food. It’s nourishment for your entire being. At Bali Yoga School, cleanliness is a reflection of inner purity. A pure plate leads to a peaceful heart. And every mindful bite is a step toward harmony.
