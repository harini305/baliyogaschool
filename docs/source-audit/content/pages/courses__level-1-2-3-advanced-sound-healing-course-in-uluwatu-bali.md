What is Sound Healing?

Sound healing is an ancient therapeutic practice that uses vibrations and frequencies to promote physical, emotional, and spiritual well being. It is an effective healing modality which operates under the fact that all things in the universe including our bodies vibrate at a particular frequency. When these natural frequencies are disturbed, sound healing therapy can be used to restore sanity and order in our body.

The practitioners produce some sound frequencies (with multiple sound instruments, including singing bowls, drums, gongs, chimes and tuning forks) and resonate with various regions of the body and energy centers. The vibrations travel deep into our cells, tissues and organs and assist in relaxing us and the natural healing powers of our body.

Sound healing is effective because it helped the patients to change their brainwaves to the alpha and theta mode, like in deep meditation. This change enables the nervous system to shift away fight-or-flight mode into rest-and-digest mode, in which actual healing can be practiced. The vibrations also aid in releasing the blockages in our energy system, clearing the stagnant emotions and opening up of space to allow in the new, positive energy.
The practice is open to all, irrespective of the musical background and experience. The healing occurs just by listening and receiving the vibrations, and it is a very gentle yet deep method of taking care of your health and well-being in our modern and sometimes chaotic world.
