Discover the art of self-healing and healing others through our life-changing 13 Days Ayurvedic Massages And
Panchakarma Therapy (AMPT) course in the enchanting island of Bali, Indonesia. Ayurvedic massages stand apart from other massages by offering a holistic approach that aims to restore not only the physical body but the mental health, applying treatments for any specific concerns when needed. Pancha, meaning five, and karmas, meaning actions, represent the ancient wisdom of Ayurvedic therapy that facilitates the removal of toxins from the body and enhances overall wellness. These actions include Vaman (emesis), Virechan (purgation), Basti (enema), Nasya (nasal instillation), and Raktamokshan (blood purification). Our course will equip you with a deep understanding of these practices, covering the fundamentals of Ayurveda and providing practical sessions on specific techniques. Embrace this transformative journey of 13 days and immerse yourself in the profound wisdom of holistic Ayurveda.
