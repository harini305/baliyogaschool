What is Reiki Healing?

Reiki are an old healing method from Japan. It is teaches by Mikao Usui in the early 1900s. The word “Reiki” means two Japanese words: Rei means “universal” and Ki means “life energy.” So Reiki be about connecting with the life energy around us and inside us. In a Reiki session, the healer places their hands lightly on or near the other person’s body. This helps energy flow to the areas that needs it most.

The receivers often explain it as warmth, tingling, or deep calm. Reiki does not use pressure or massage. It is soft, gentle, and very peaceful. The best part is – anyone can learn it. You don’t need to believe in any spiritual things, and it also work well when combined with meditation, yoga asanas, or therapy. In our Level 1/2 Reiki Healing Course, you will learn the basics of energy, how to heal yourself, how to give Reiki to others, and even how to send healing energy across distance. You also get attuned by a Reiki Master so you can channel this energy.
