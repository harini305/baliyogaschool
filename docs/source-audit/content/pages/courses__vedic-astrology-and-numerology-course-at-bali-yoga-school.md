What Is Vedic Astrology?

The Vedic Astrology, also known as Jyotish Shastra, is considered one of the world's oldest, and most trusted spiritual sciences. Jyotish comes from the Vedic era of ancient India, and it literally means the "science of light. It explores the correlation between movement of the planets and human life, and how it relates to personality, strengths, challenges, opportunities, and karmic patterns.

Unlike generalised horoscope readings commonly published in the popular media, Vedic Astrology is based on the exact date, time and place of birth to create a detailed birth chart called a Janma Kundli. This chart acts as a sort of cosmic blueprint that charts out some of the important influences throughout a person's life journey.

The relative positions of the planets at the time of birth provide clues about the following areas of life:

Career planning and development
Relationships and marriage
Money trends and wealth.
Physical and mental health and welfare
Spiritual development
The purpose of life and the meaning of destiny.
Recurring lessons and challenges for Karmic lessons.

Students learn to understand these influences from detailed analysis, and use them as practical advice to become more aligned with their potential.

Understanding Numerology

Another ancient metaphysical science is numerology, the study of the vibrations of numbers and the influence that they have on the human experience. Each number carries a distinct energy and affects aspects of personality, decision-making, relationship and life experiences in general.

Numerology can provide insight into a person's birth date and name and indicate:

Life Path Number
Destiny Number
Soul Urge Number
Personality Number
Personal Year Cycles
Natural strengths and talents
Underlying challenges
Relationship compatibility

Vedic Astrology combined with Numerology gives more information, which helps in having a complete picture of a person's life journey and the pattern that influences it.
