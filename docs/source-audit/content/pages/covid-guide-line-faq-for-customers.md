Bali Travel Regulations & Travel Requirements

Indonesia has recently allowed visa on arrival to tourist visiting Bali. You can check below the requirement and eligibility for Visa on arrival.

1 – In order to visit Indonesia and Bali all international travellers need to have a status of being “Fully Vaccinated”.

2-  Travellers from below mentioned 23 nationalities can obtain a VISA ON ARRIVAL (VoA) – ONLY if they arrive directly in Bali.

List of nationalities that can obtain a Visa on Arrival:

Australia,USA, Canada, UK, Germany, Netherlands, France ,Qatar, Japan, South Korea, Italy, New Zealand, Turkey, UAE Malaysia, Singapore, Brunei, Thailand, Vietnam, Laos, Myanmar, Cambodia, Philippines

 3- Must know about the Visa on Arrival:

You will have to purchase a visa sticker in the arrival hall at the airport in DPS before you queue at the immigration counter

valid for 30 days, and can be extended ONCE for 30 days, after that you will have to leave the country. You cannot apply for a B211A afterwards.

Will only be issued in Bali, no other airports

Costs: IDR 500,000 (ca USD 35) (USD, EUR, AUD cash or Visa, MasterCard) Only for Tourism Purpose

Return ticket needed

Make sure passport is still valid at least 6 months from the day you arrive in Indonesia

 4 – Who need B211 e – visa?

B211A Visa is required –

If you are entering Indonesia NOT via Bali, Denpasar

If you wish to stay longer than 60 days

If you travel with a business purpose (meetings, sourcing, etc)

If your nationality is not listed in the list of eligible nationalities

The B211A that we facilitate requires a company or travel agency to “sponsor” the visa, which is included in our service.

 5- Who can apply for a Visa B211A?

Travellers from almost all countries can apply for a visit e-visa.

But, due to pre-existing travel restrictions nationals from following countries CAN NOT apply for visit e-visas for Indonesia, but have to contact directly their embassy,

 Afghanistan, Guinea, Israel, Kamerun, Liberia, Nigeria, North Korea, Somalia

Some travellers from Iraq and Iran also have difficulties to get the visa approved.

Can I catch a direct flight to Bali?

Yes, there are airlines directly flying to Bali but not many airlines are offering this destination yet. Airlines are still adjusting and will gradually include Bali as a “direct destination”. And because they can only allow the foreigners coming from these 23 countries above to arrive directly in Bali, they have the challenge to get enough passengers. Simply check with flight ticket sites of your choice.

How is the situation in Bali?

The situation is overall well under control. The number of vaccinated citizens is above 80%. The government still applies a cautious approach and keeps Micro-Scale Social Restrictions (PPKM Regulations) in place, to mitigate the spread of Covid-19. Expect at times shorter operational hours on restaurants and attractions, also health protocols enforcement

Wearing masks in Public is mandatory?

You need the PeduliLindungi App when visiting some venues and stores. The app is still getting ready to work also for foreigners properly.
