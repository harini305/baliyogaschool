What is Sound Healing?

Sound healing is a way to use sound and vibrations to make your body, mind, and soul healthy. People have used sound for thousands of years for healing. The Tibetan bowls sound has always helps the people feels good and connect with their spirit. Sound healing uses instruments like Tibetan singing bowls, gong, drums, chimes, tuning forks, rain sound stick and even the human voice.

These instruments make vibrations that go deep inside to our body. As our body is mostly water, and water carries vibrations very well. When healing sounds reach us, they move through the water in our body, calm our nerves, relax our mind, and balance our energy.

In today’s busy world, sound healing gives peace. It is a gentle way to reduce stress and tension. Science also shows that some sound frequencies can lower stress hormones, improve sleep, and make people feel better. At BYS, our courses make this ancient practice easy for everyone. You don’t need to be a musician or have experience.

Just have an open heart and a wish to explore the power of sound..
