9 Days Yoga Retreat in Canggu Bali 

A walk by the seaside watching the serene sunset, a cup of cappuccino in a cozy café, or an exciting adventure while surfing – Canggu is becoming an emerging hotspot and sought-after destination for travelers all across the globe. And the presence of a growing number of yoga studios and retreat centers has made it a convenient affair for those passionate about both yoga and travel and who would ideally like to have the best of both worlds! The 9 days yoga retreat in Canggu Bali is the ultimate treat for yogi travelers and a great way to obtain some quality time and space for yourselfyour! The coastal zone of Canggu with its serene vibes, warm weather and pristine beaches perfectly favor the learning and practice of yoga while allowing the right ambience to focus deeply within while practicing the asanas or meditating. This is the reason that makes the 9 days yoga retreat in Canggu Bali  the right choice for yogi travelers. You will instantly feel the vibes as you step foot into this tropical paradise on the south coast of Bali.  And to add the element of convenience and comfort to your holiday plan, Bali Yoga School offers the ultimate “all-in-one” package including food, accommodation, excursions, daily yoga classes, ayurvedic massage sessions, detox and de-stress therapies and much more within their 9 days yoga retreat in Canggu Bali. So just sit back and relax and join this elite yoga school in Bali for a meaningful and enjoyable “9 – day break” well spent.

Our teachers possess the ancient knowledge and modern yoga teaching methods to offer classes for beginner, intermediate and advanced levels for yoga. We offer yoga sessions twice a daily and a meditation session. We also offer customised yoga retreats for those who want to delve deeper into the ancient spiritual yoga tradition and allow you to experience the physical & subtle aspects of yoga. Bali offers a great yoga experience; it has a number of resorts, waterfalls, temples, shops, and much more. We guarantee you a completely refreshing experience in yoga school in Bali. A yoga retreat is a perfect way to take a break and divert your mind from negative thoughts to get settle into a blissful conscious state.
