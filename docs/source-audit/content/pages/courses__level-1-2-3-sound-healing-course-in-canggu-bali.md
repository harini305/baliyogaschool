What is Sound Healing?

Sound healing is a natural way of treatment, which makes use of certain vibrations and frequencies to bring balance and encourage healing at a physical, emotional, and spiritual level. This powerful practice is founded on the central assumption that everything in this world, such as our bodies, vibrates at a particular frequency. Sickness, stress, or imbalance of emotions disrupt these natural frequencies, and sound healing is employed to assist in returning the harmony and the most desirable state of vibration.

Therapeutic sound frequencies are created by practitioners using carefully selected instruments, including singing bowls, gongs, drums, chimes, flutes, and tuning forks. Each instrument possesses its vibrations that vibrate with the body and energy centres. Such vibrations pass through our tissues, organs and energy channels and stimulate the natural healing system of our body and bring a state of deep relaxation and restoration.

Effectiveness of sound healing is a feature that helps direct the brainwaves to alpha and theta levels, the ones that can be achieved when one is either in deep meditation or in a deep sleep. This change in the nervous system permits the nervous system to move out of the stress-related sympathetic system to the parasympathetic system where the actual healing and regeneration can take place. The vibrations of the therapeutic wash over and pass through the body, dissolving any energy blockages, tensions as well as emotional stagnation, making room to allow new vitality and well being to flow into the body..
