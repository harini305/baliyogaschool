5 DAYS & 4 NIGHTS YOGA RETREAT IN UBUD BALI

Combine your holiday with yoga and bring your near and dear ones along with you for the best yoga retreat in Bali. Unwind yourself under the sun in our gardens with 2 swimming pools surrounded by the green, as you enjoy each and every moment during your Yoga holiday in Bali. Let Bali Yoga School – the top leading yoga school in Bali to offer you a 5-day long yoga retreat program to make your rejuvenation time in Bali really special.

Our yoga retreat center is located in heart of Bali (Ubud) that has a calm environment. You will be staying our private room where you can unwind your self and be totally free to relax on your own. You will be having daily yoga classes as well as daily healing classes and Balinese massage treatment in our spa. Yoga instructors at Bali Yoga School teach authentic and personalised yoga lessons according to your condition and experience. The 5 day yoga retreat in Bali enables you to integrate the benefits of authentic yoga practices with our professional sound and reiki healers.

Our teachers possess the ancient knowledge and modern yoga teaching methods to offer classes for beginner, intermediate and advanced levels for yoga practitioners. Bali offers a great yoga experience; it has a lot of healthy cafes with vegan and vegetarian options, beaches and waterfalls, temples, spiritual events, and much more. We guarantee you a completely refreshing experience with us at our yoga school in Bali. The yoga retreat is a perfect way to take a break and divert your mind from negative thoughts to get settle into a blissful conscious state.

The best and most convenient way to travel to Bali Yoga School is to take a flight to Denpasar Bali airport. Denpasar Bali airport is merely 38 km away from our school. Bali Yoga School Dining

We Bali Yoga School believe that food is a sacred expression of care and one of the ways to enhance our spirit. We serve is plant-based meals, prepared with the highest standards of hygiene, cleanliness, and love to support your yoga journey. Yoga is a way of living, and we bring that teaching in the meals as well. Our every dish reflects our commitment to provide the best yoga training experience, reflecting purity and mindful living. Our dedicated team prepares food with intention, mindfulness, and a deep sense of respect for the process. At Bali Yoga School, cleanliness is a reflection of inner purity. A pure plate leads to a peaceful heart. And every mindful bite is a step toward harmony.
