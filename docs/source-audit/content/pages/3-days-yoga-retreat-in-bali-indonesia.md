MORE THAN A YOGA HOLIDAY
A Short Retreat with Space to Move, Slow Down and Reconnect

This 3-day yoga and healing retreat in Ubud is designed for travellers who want meaningful rest without committing to a long programme or yoga teacher training course.

Your mornings create energy through meditation and breath-led Vinyasa Flow. Your healing sessions invite quiet rest, while Balinese and Ayurvedic massage give the body time to soften after travel and movement. Fresh plant-based meals, an unhurried campus rhythm and free time by the pool complete the experience.

There is no pressure to perform and no need to arrive flexible. The retreat simply gives you a supportive setting to listen to your body, steady your attention and return to your Bali journey feeling refreshed.

PRACTICE WITH PURPOSE
Vinyasa, Meditation and Reiki in a Supportive Setting

The programme is intentionally approachable. Each practice supports a different quality—energy, attention or rest—without turning your retreat into an intensive training.

ENERGISE
Breath-Led Vinyasa Flow Yoga

Vinyasa Flow connects movement with the natural rhythm of your breath. Guided sequences invite you to move through standing postures, gentle transitions, mobility work and grounded stretches with steady attention.

Your teacher can offer variations so that beginners feel supported while regular practitioners stay engaged. The aim is not to achieve a perfect pose; it is to build warmth, awaken the body and experience movement with greater awareness.

TWO MASSAGE TRADITIONS
From Arrival Tension to Deeper Rest

Rather than repeating the same treatment, the retreat introduces two distinct massage experiences at thoughtful points in your schedule.

Two treatments are placed at different points in the retreat so each one supports the natural rhythm of your stay.
DAY 1 · ARRIVE & RELEASE
Balinese Massage

Begin with a relaxing Balinese massage after check-in and travel. Flowing strokes and traditional bodywork techniques create a gentle transition from the pace of your journey into a quieter retreat rhythm.

DAY 2 · RESTORE MORE DEEPLY
Ayurvedic Massage

Receive an Ayurvedic-inspired massage after a full day of yoga, meditation and Reiki. This second treatment offers dedicated time for physical rest and complements the slower, restorative focus of the programme.

Massage sessions are wellness experiences, not medical treatments. Please tell the team about pregnancy, allergies, recent surgery, injuries or pressure preferences before your appointment.
