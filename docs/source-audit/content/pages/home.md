Founder of Bali Yoga School

Himanshu Joshi

Everything you experience at Bali Yoga School community has come from our great leader, Himanshu Joshi. He is the most honest, hard-working, authentic person we can ever meet. His yoga journey started from a severe injury after playing sports for a long time at the state / national level. Yoga brought him up into the light from the darkness.

He contributed to the success of other yoga schools in Rishikesh, and then built his own — Himalayan Yoga Association and Bali Yoga School. He continues to expand the community, uplifting lives for the better. Feel his essence in every detail of the school and learning.

Why Join Bali Yoga School

We are recognized as the best yoga school in Bali for yoga teacher training course & yoga retreat in Bali, With years of profound experience listening to the needs of yoga students, we continue to help yoga learners in making yoga accessible. We have the most experienced yoga teachers and instructors from around the world who make your stay at Bali Yoga School unforgettable.

We have also designed customized yoga retreat packages for your personalized needs. Our yoga teacher training in Bali and yoga retreat offers a number of courses for those who want to become a yoga teacher in the future. We start planning from the day, you join us. We take care of your food preferences, favorite color, and activity level to offer you the best experience. So, what are you waiting for? Join a yoga course to explore yoga and yourself in Bali.

Courses We Offer – 50/ 100/ 200/ 300/ 500 Hour Ashtanga Vinyasa & Hatha Vinyasa Based Yoga Teacher Training Courses in Bali, Yoga Retreats In Bali, Sound Healing Courses In Bali, Reiki Healing Courses in Bali, Ayurveda Courses In Bali, 50/ 100/ 200/ 300/ 500 Hour Yin Yoga Based Yoga Teacher Training Courses in Bali, Online Yoga Teacher Training Courses.

Location– Bali yoga school is located in Ubud (the heart of bali), Very peaceful location with complete home feeling, 10 mins walking distance to ubud market, cafes, temples, waterfalls, beach are close to the school.

Accommodation– The accommodation offers guests a tranquil, rejuvenating, home away from home experience, each room is spacious and has its own private bathroom with all modern western facilities, we have also outdoor swimming pool, Wifi, filtered water, tea facility and so on.
