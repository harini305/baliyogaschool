A PURPOSEFUL SHORT RETREAT

Movement opens the door. Stillness lets you listen.

This 4-day yoga retreat in Ubud, Bali is designed as a clear journey—not a crowded wellness package and not an intensive Yoga Teacher Training. Each day connects mindful movement, meditation and conscious sound so you have time to practise, pause and absorb the experience.

Arrive and ground on Day 1, build awareness through movement and breath on Day 2, receive the signature sound experience on Day 3, then integrate your practice before departure on Day 4. Spacious breaks for swimming, journaling and rest keep the rhythm supportive rather than demanding.
