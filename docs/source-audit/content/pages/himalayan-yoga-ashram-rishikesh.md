01 · OUR IDENTITY

About Himalayan Yoga Ashram

Himalayan Yoga Ashram is a yoga, wellness, and conscious living destination in Rishikesh, India, and is part of the Himalayan Yoga Association community.

Established alongside Himalayan Yoga Association in 2017, Himalayan Yoga Ashram has grown as a space where students, yoga practitioners, wellness travellers, and spiritual seekers from around the world can experience yoga and mindful living in the birthplace of yoga.

Through the Himalayan Yoga Association community, the ashram is connected with yoga education, meditation, wellness, healing practices, vegetarian food, accommodation, and community experiences in Rishikesh.

This page has been created on the Bali Yoga School website to introduce Himalayan Yoga Ashram, explain its connection with Himalayan Yoga Association and Bali Yoga School, and provide clear information to help visitors identify and reach the correct Himalayan Yoga Ashram in Rishikesh, India.

Himalayan Yoga Ashram and Himalayan Yoga Association

The journey of Himalayan Yoga Ashram and Himalayan Yoga Association began in 2017 with a vision of creating an environment where traditional yoga knowledge could be shared with students from different countries and backgrounds.

Over the years, this community has expanded beyond yoga teacher training to include wellness, healing, food, hospitality, and spaces designed to support a more balanced and conscious lifestyle.

Students visiting Himalayan Yoga Ashram can experience an environment connected to the broader Himalayan Yoga Association community while staying in Rishikesh, one of the world's best-known destinations for yoga, meditation, and spiritual learning.

The relationship is simple: Himalayan Yoga Ashram is a branch and residential campus of Himalayan Yoga Association in Rishikesh, India. This distinction is important for students and travellers searching online for our school, accommodation, courses, wellness services, or location.
