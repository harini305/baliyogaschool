12-Day, 50-Hour Prenatal & Postnatal Yoga Teacher Training in Bali, Indonesia

This 12-day Prenatal & Postnatal Yoga Teacher Training Course in Bali guides you through anatomy, birth philosophy, Ayurveda, and ancient knowledge which can help mother and child to be healthy. The course will help you to guide women through the birthing process calmly and confidently. It also delves into post-partum depression and the methods of meditation that assist mothers to soothe their body and mind after birth. It is a wonderful opportunity for any yogi who wishes to help others and women in the world. We invite you to join us in Bali to discover this ancient wisdom and apply it to help mothers navigate one of life’s most significant changes. 

At Bali Yoga School, we built an entire course around it because we saw how underserved this area really is. Teachers leave standard 200-hour training without ever having received training on how to adapt a pose for a woman in her 3rd trimester, or how to talk to a new mom who is emotionally unwell after childbirth. We were hoping to do something about that. This course is designed for yoga teachers, doulas, health care professionals and mothers themselves to be able to enter into this work realistically, not randomly.

What Will You Learn & Why Choose This Course?
What Will You Learn in This Course?
Yoga Philosophy of Pregnancy: We examine pregnancy from the classical yogic perspective, and understand how the mind, body and spirit work together during this phase, and how this will impact teaching.
Reproductive Anatomy and Physiology: A strong foundation of the physical changes a woman experiences so that every adjustment and pose you teach is based on knowledge, not assumption.
Instead of a single sequence, you will learn different sets of postures that are appropriate for the prenatal and postnatal body, working on strength, flexibility and body awareness for each stage.
Nutrition Through Pregnancy: How food affects the mother's health and her baby's growth and development, and suggestions for what to eat and what to avoid.
Garbhopanishad: One of the more interesting aspects of this training, this ancient scripture gives an unusually detailed account of the spiritual and biological progress of a fetus and the lessons contained within it are still relevant today.
Prenayama for Prenatal Support: Learn unique breathing techniques to release stress from the body, quieten the emotions and connect mother and baby during pregnancy.
Pre and Postnatal Care Psychotherapy: Understand the significance of emotional and psychological health and healing during the period of pregnancy and postpartum healing, and what yoga teachers can do to be able to hold space compassionately.
Practices for connecting to the unborn child, utilizing sound and stillness, with a conscious and sacred relationship for the mother prior to the birth of her child, Mantra, Meditation and Connection with the Unborn Child.
Teaching Methodology: The actual process of arranging, organizing and modifying prenatal and post natal classes in a safe, clear and supportive way, and the process of being able to confidently communicate with a client who may be vulnerable or uncertain.
Why Choose This Pre & Postnatal Yoga TTC at Bali Yoga School?
Rooted in Tradition and Modern Knowledge: This course doesn't pick a side between ancient yogic wisdom and modern medical knowledge. It combines them together so you can walk out of the door with a grounded and up-to-date perspective.
Experienced Teachers: This means your yoga teachers here are both practising yoga teachers and medical practitioners, not manual readers! They take real life experiences to each session.
Learning by Doing: This training is a lot about doing things with the students rather than just lectures. You depart with knowledge of how to teach this, having practiced teaching it with feedback in between to hone your skills.
A Network, Not Just a Certificate: You will be working with other teachers, mothers (or healers) who also have the same aim. A lot of past students keep in touch with each other long after the course is over, creating a network of support after 12 days.
Training and certification in Bali: Completed your training and are now certified in Bali, surrounded by rice paddies and the calm Bali is known for, an appropriate place to mark this milestone and contemplate on all you have learnt.
Who Is This Course For?

This training is built for a wide range of people, including:

Pregnant women, whether early in pregnancy or further along, who want to feel more prepared and grounded for what's ahead, and who want practical tools they can use on their own.
Partners who want to go through this experience together, learning side by side rather than from the sidelines, so they can actively support rather than just watch from a distance.
Yoga teachers who want to add this specialisation to what they already offer, opening up a niche that's still underserved in most studios.
Health professionals, nutritionists, and Ayurveda practitioners looking to fold yoga into their existing work with pregnant and postpartum clients, adding a holistic layer to the care they already provide.
Anyone who feels pulled toward this kind of work, even without formal yoga experience, including doulas, midwives, and those simply drawn to supporting women through this stage of life.
As a yoga trainer learning from us, we are here to assist you in not only increasing your confidence in the way you do yoga, but also in your capacity to connect with the women you will be training. It's not a physical or clinical ability that supports a mother while she is pregnant. It takes compassion, sensitivity and a woman's understanding that goes beyond the technical and reaches the emotional, spiritual level.
